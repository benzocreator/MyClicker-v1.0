MYCLICKER – FAST AUTO CLICKER
=============================

Thank you for downloading MyClicker!

DESCRIPTION:
MyClicker is a lightweight, high-precision auto clicker for Windows. It can click up to 1000 CPS, supports a customizable hotkey, allows you to choose Left/Right mouse button, and has Randomized CPS for natural clicks. A CPS Overlay shows your real click speed in real time.

HOW TO RUN:
1. Double-click MyClicker.exe to start.
2. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC":
   - Click "More info"
   - Then click "Run anyway"
3. Set your desired CPS and mouse button.
4. Press the hotkey (default F6) to toggle auto-clicking.

SAFETY:
- MyClicker contains no viruses.
- It does not collect any data.
- Works completely offline.
- Only automates mouse clicks for your convenience.

CUSTOMIZATION:
- Change the hotkey: Click "Choose Hotkey" in the interface.
- Randomize CPS: Enable "Randomize CPS ±3" for more natural clicking.
- Toggle overlay: Click "Toggle CPS Overlay" to show/hide real-time CPS.
